# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Recommended Structure and Order

### 1. Project Title / Headline

# HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and organizational performance. The dashboard enables HR professionals to identify workforce trends, improve employee retention, and make data-driven human resource decisions.

---

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI report designed to help HR managers and business leaders monitor employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, job satisfaction, and departmental performance. The dashboard provides meaningful insights that support strategic workforce planning, employee engagement, and retention initiatives.

---

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

• 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Used to design and develop interactive reports and dashboards.<br>

• 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation before visualization.<br>

• 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create KPIs, calculated columns, measures, and dynamic calculations.<br>

• 📝 **Data Modeling** – Built relationships between employee and HR-related tables to enable efficient filtering and analysis.<br>

• 📈 **Microsoft Excel** – Used as the primary data source for employee records.<br>

• 📁 **File Formats** – `.pbix` for dashboard development, `.xlsx` for the dataset, and `.png` for dashboard preview images.

---

### 4. Data Source

**Source:** HR Employee Dataset

The dataset contains information for **1,470 employees**, including employee demographics, education, department, job role, salary, age, marital status, business travel, job satisfaction, and attrition status.

The data is used to analyze employee turnover, workforce distribution, job satisfaction, and organizational performance.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations because it increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and impacts overall business performance.

HR managers often struggle to answer important business questions such as:

- Which department has the highest employee attrition?
- Which job roles experience the highest turnover?
- Which age group is most likely to leave the organization?
- Does education background influence employee attrition?
- How satisfied are employees across different job roles?
- Which workforce segments require better retention strategies?

Answering these questions using raw HR data is time-consuming and difficult.

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide an interactive HR analytics solution that:

- Monitors employee attrition across the organization.
- Analyzes workforce demographics and employee distribution.
- Tracks job satisfaction across different job roles.
- Identifies departments with high employee turnover.
- Supports HR managers in making data-driven workforce decisions.
- Improves employee retention through actionable insights.

---

- **Active Employees:** 1,233
- **Average Employee Age:** 37 Years
- **Average Monthly Salary:** 6,503

---

#### 🎓 Education Filter

Interactive slicers allow users to filter employee data by education level:

- Associate Degree
- Bachelor's Degree
- Master's Degree
- Doctoral Degree
- High School

---

#### 👥 Employee Demographics Filter

Users can interactively filter employees by:

- Gender
- Marital Status
- Business Travel

This enables detailed workforce segmentation.

---

#### 📊 Employees by Age Group & Gender

A clustered column chart compares the number of male and female employees across different age groups, helping HR understand workforce demographics.

---

#### 📈 Attrition by Age

A line chart visualizes employee attrition across different age groups, allowing HR teams to identify which age ranges experience the highest turnover.

---

#### 🏢 Department-wise Attrition

A donut chart compares employee attrition across departments, including:

- Human Resources
- Sales
- Research & Development

This helps identify departments requiring retention initiatives.

---

#### 💼 Attrition Rate by Job Role

A horizontal bar chart ranks job roles based on attrition rate, enabling HR managers to identify positions with the highest employee turnover.

---

#### 😊 Job Satisfaction Rating

A matrix visualization displays employee satisfaction ratings across different job roles, providing valuable insights into workforce engagement.

---

#### 🎓 Attrition by Education Field

A funnel chart illustrates employee attrition by education background, highlighting which educational fields contribute most to employee turnover.
